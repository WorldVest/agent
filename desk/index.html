A private desk for a few friends who follow markets

WorldVest started as a shared watchlist and chat for a few friends. This page adds an analyst to the room.

Ask her about the week ahead, why a stock moved, or how a strategy works, from covered calls to position sizing. She answers with real numbers from our watchlist and current news, and she can go as deep as you like.

There is one shared conversation. Everyone in the group sees it, and she remembers the last 12 messages, so she can pick up a thread someone else started yesterday.

Her answers are analysis to think with, not personal financial advice.

What she works from

Our watchlist
Every ticker we track, with live prices. Stocks update about every 30 seconds, crypto about every 5 minutes.

The top market stories of the day, pulled fresh each time you ask.
Web search
For anything newer than her training: earnings dates, Fed meetings, CPI and jobs reports, breaking news.
The conversation
The last 12 messages from everyone, so follow-up questions just work.
