About

Built from inside insurance.

WorldVest comes from inside insurance, where money between businesses still takes days to arrive and weeks to reconcile. We're building the layer that makes it fast, matched and provable. The live demo is our first step.

Built for

Insurance money moves between a lot of hands.

Premiums, commissions and claims pass through agencies, captives and administrators before they land, and every handoff is another payment to send, wait on and match.

  • Agencies

    Collect premium and pass it on.

  • Captives

    Hold the risk and fund the claims.

  • Administrators

    Process the claims and pay them out.
